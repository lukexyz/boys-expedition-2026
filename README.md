# Dolomites 2026 — The Boys' Expedition

Trip planning for six of us in the Dolomites, 18–23 August 2026.
Five hikes, three basecamps, one hire car.

`index.html` is the interactive map: a top nav steps through each day, updating
routes, photo cards and where you're sleeping that night. `bento.html` is the
same trip as a five-chapter scrolling narrative with the map fixed alongside,
following you down the page.

## The trip

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hike 1** | Nuvolau & Averau loop from Passo Giau — 8.8 km / 620 m |
| **Hike 2** | Lagazuoi WWI tunnels *or* Piz Boè 3,152 m summit — undecided, both drawn |
| **Hike 3** | Tre Cime di Lavaredo loop + Cadini di Misurina — date-locked to Thu 20 Aug |
| **Hike 4** | Torri del Vajolet to Rifugio Re Alberto — 14.7 km / 950 m |
| **Hike 5** | Seceda ridgeline |

Nights: San Vito di Cadore (1–3) → Rifugio Re Alberto (4) → Rifugio Fermeda (5).

## Notes

Routes are indicative lines drawn through the real waypoints for orientation —
they are **not** surveyed GPX. Download the proper tracks from the linked guides
and carry them offline.

`MUSINGS.md` has the full working notes: how the schedule was reconciled against
the bookings, every design decision, and what broke along the way.
