CHAPTER 05The Finale Saturday 22 – Sunday 23
Hike 5
Saturday · late light

The Seceda ridgeline

Descend the Vajolet, collect the car, drive north to Ortisei, then ride to 2,510m and walk the tilted knife-edge with the Odle group filling the sky. Easy on paper — the first viewpoint is exposed with no rail.
